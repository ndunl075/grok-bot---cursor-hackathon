# weekly-retro

Sunday 18:00. Always sends.

Four lines, per daily-brief's retro shape:
1. Grade deltas this week, largest first.
2. Highest-impact item in the next 7 days.
3. At-risk course, or "nothing at risk."
4. One concrete action.

This is the routine that makes the bot feel like it's paying attention over
time rather than just reacting. Do not let it grow past four lines.
